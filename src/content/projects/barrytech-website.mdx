## The goal

Give a technology company a clear online presence where visitors can understand its services and find a relevant next step.

## What I built

The website organizes company information and services, with a service assessment flow that lets visitors select needs and submit an enquiry.

## Tools and decisions

The project uses React, Vite, Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion. Service content is structured separately from presentation, and the assessment flow connects to form and email services.
